Privacy Policy

Eat Up! Hide Grandma's Food (com.sveteroc.babushka)

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game "Eat Up! Hide Grandma's Food" (package com.sveteroc.babushka), developed by Sveteroc ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the game.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The game does not require registration or login. Your game progress, coins, settings and statistics are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language);
  • ad interaction data (ad views and clicks).

This information is processed by Google according to its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → "Ad privacy settings".

4. Your choices

You can reset or limit the use of your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). You can stop all data processing by uninstalling the game.

5. Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data security

Since we do not collect personal data, no personal data is stored on our servers. Data collected by Google AdMob is protected by Google's security measures.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact us
